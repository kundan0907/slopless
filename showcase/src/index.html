Design thatlooks decided.

slopless is a skill for Claude Code and Codex. It asks what you are trying to say, picks a direction with you, builds with real motion, and checks its own work before you see it.

Six steps, in this order.

  1. Interview

    Story first: who is in the room, the one sentence they should leave with, and the object that travels through it. Then taste. Four questions at a time, each with a recommended answer.

    How much should it move?

    Stillprint quality
    Subtleentrances, feedback
    Cinematicrecommended for a demo
    Spatialreal 3D, WebGL

  2. Direction

    An inventory of things only your subject has, one idea sentence that fails if a competitor's name is swapped in, a colour strategy before colours, and type chosen on purpose.

    On this deck, the support ticket becomes the thread that carries every slide.

    Sapphire GlassRestrainedBricolage / HankenCinematic

  3. Prove

    Two small live studies on real content that differ in material and choreography. You pick one before anyone builds thirty slides on a guess.

    Study AGlass stage, device carries the story
    Study BMatte paper, type-led evidence

  4. Build

    Tokens first, then the opening, then every part on the same system. Still first, motion second: one timeline, every state seekable, reduced motion lands each beat.

    :root {
  --ground: #070B18;  --ink: #E8EEFA;
  --accent: #9DB7FF;  --panel: #0F1730;
}
tl.addLabel("ticket-claimed")
  .to(ticket, { x: () => dock.x, ease: "power3.inOut" })

  5. Verify

    A scanner for the tells, screenshots at every size in a real browser, a critique against ten passes, one batch of fixes. It says what it checked and what it did not.

    FAIL deck.html  [violet-gradient] 2 violet/indigo gradient(s)
FAIL deck.html  [copy] stock words: seamless, unlock
WARN deck.html  [eyebrow] 3 tiny tracked-uppercase label style(s)

1 file(s): 0 FAIL, 0 WARN — clean of banned tells

  6. Learn

    Every correction you make becomes one dated line in your taste file. The next project starts from what you already said you like.

    2026-11-02Loves frosted glass on story cards, opaque panels for evidence.
    2026-11-02Rejects decorative colour bars. “Looks generated.”
    2026-11-03Wants two live studies before a full build.

How it differs.

slopless stands on other people's work. This is what it took from each project, and what it adds. It is not a benchmark: we have not run these against each other, and each project has moved since NOTICE was written. Read their own pages for what they do today.

With no skill

  • The model alone

    Left to its defaults it produces the average of everything it has read: centred hero, eyebrow label, three icon cards, a violet gradient. Nothing checks the result or remembers what you said last time.

What slopless adapted

  • Impeccable

    Craft floor checks, refinement versus redesign, colour strategies, training-data font defaults, bounded verification passes, and the bolder, quieter, distill and harden modes. Apache-2.0.

  • taste-skill

    The subject inventory and idea sentence, the competitor test, one live thing, a dated tells registry, "removal is not enough" and the look-and-fix screenshot loop. MIT.

  • UI/UX Pro Max

    Priority-ordered UX rules, platform touch targets and native-app polish, a persisted design-system pattern with master and overrides, and density and motion dials. MIT.

What slopless adds

  • Story-first interview

    Round one asks what it is, who sees it and the one sentence, before any taste question. interview.md

  • Presenter-controlled decks

    Arrow keys play the next beat and stop; the presenter owns the pace. deck.md

  • Taste memory

    Your corrections become dated lines in a file that outranks the skill's defaults on the next run. memory.md

  • Opt-in agents with model tiers

    Offered once on big jobs, one to four helpers, cheaper models for mechanical work. agents.md

  • Glass when you choose it

    Banned as decoration, allowed as a chosen material with a recipe. tells.md

  • One router, references on demand

    A short SKILL.md loads one small file per step, so a small fix never pulls in the library. SKILL.md

  • A bundled scanner

    The same file runs from the command line and in the scanner above. slop-scan.mjs

Decks, sites, apps and graphics.

Agents, when the job is big.

Off by default. For a thirty-slide deck or a full site, slopless offers it once and you choose how many helpers. Screenshots and scans run on the cheaper models; only the signature move gets the expensive one.

    What's in the skill.

    Every file, one line each, linked to the source. A run loads only the rows whose "Loaded" column matches the step it is on.

    Where it looks.

    When you want references, or a technique needs researching, slopless points here. The rule is to learn the technique and never clone the site.

    Reading

    To research a technique: open it in a real browser and note what moves, what triggers it and what happens on a phone. Inspect how it is built. Write it down as a decision before coding, then say what was adopted and what was built.

    Prompts to copy.

    Name the skill, say what you are making, and say what must not change. Anything you leave out, slopless asks.

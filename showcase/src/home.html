Make AI-built UIlook designed.

slopless gives your coding agent a design language, with a palette, type and motion chosen for your subject. Then it checks what it built, so your pages, decks and app screens stop looking generated.

How it works.

  1. 1Ask

    It asks about your story and your taste first, and suggests an answer to every question.

  2. 2Design

    It picks a direction, palette, type and motion that fit your subject, then builds.

  3. 3Check

    It scans and screenshots its own work, fixes what looks generated, and remembers what you like.

On a big job it can also split the work across helpers (agents mode).

What it fixes.

The usual giveaways of an AI-made interface, and what takes their place.

  • Violet gradient wallpaperOne palette chosen for your subject
  • Emoji standing in for iconsDrawn icons from one set
  • Tiny uppercase label above the headlineA headline that says it
  • Stock words like unlock and seamlessPlain, specific copy
  • A glow on everythingDepth only where it helps
  • The same default font everywhereType picked on purpose

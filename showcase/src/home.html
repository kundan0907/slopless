How it works.

  1. 1Inspect

    For existing work, Quick starts from your content and the real screen. New work resolves missing story and taste decisions.

  2. 2Improve

    Quick makes a clear visual upgrade and checks it before and after. Standard and Deep explore a new direction when the job needs one.

  3. 3Check

    It checks the rendering, content and controls, repairs concrete problems, and remembers your explicit preferences.

Say “slopless quick” for a focused V2. No manual project setup; browser downloads, when needed, are cached automatically. Helpers stay opt-in.

What it fixes.

The usual giveaways of an AI-made interface, and what takes their place.

  • Violet gradient wallpaperOne palette chosen for your subject
  • Emoji standing in for iconsDrawn icons from one set
  • Tiny uppercase label above the headlineA headline that says it
  • Stock words like unlock and seamlessPlain, specific copy
  • A glow on everythingDepth only where it helps
  • The same default font everywhereType picked on purpose

Install options.

Choose your agent

Run the installer in your project and choose your coding agent when prompted. The installer requires Node.js 22.20+; you can also copy the skill folder by hand.

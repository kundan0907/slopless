What your agent reads.

The skill is a short router plus small files, loaded one step at a time. Pick a job to see exactly which files the agent opens, in order, with the opening lines of each, taken from the real files at build time.

Pick a mode.

Choose what you want to do, such as build a deck, review a page or make it bolder. You see what it does, how to ask for it and which files it uses.

Agents mode

Off by default. On a big job, such as a thirty-slide deck or a full site, slopless offers it once and you choose how many helpers. Checks run on cheaper models; only the signature move gets the expensive one.

    Assets it uses.

    Real icons, fonts, photos and device frames, fetched by assets.mjs instead of drawn by hand or hotlinked. The icons below were fetched when this page was built and are inlined, so the page works offline.

    Drag to compare.

    Four jobs, each built twice: the generated look on the left of the handle, the decided version on the right, in the palette you picked. Sample content, to be replaced with yours.

    How it compares.

    slopless stands on other people's work. This is what it took from each project and what it adds. It is not a benchmark: nothing was run head to head, and each project has moved since.

    Reading

    To research a technique: open it in a real browser and note what moves, what triggers it and what happens on a phone. Inspect how it is built. Write it down as a decision before coding, then say what was adopted and what was built.

    Prompts to copy.

    Name the skill, say what you are making, and say what must not change. Anything you leave out, slopless asks.

## Existing deck / Quick

Preserve its content, navigation, print output, notes, offline assets and intended slide order. Fix the responsible styles/components; do not rebuild its engine to change its look. Inspect the opening, densest slide and one dif...

## Story and composition

- One claim per view, with its supporting evidence and caveats findable. Use `../storytelling.md` when changing the argument's visual form.
- Use a 1600×900 or 1920×1080 stage only when it suits the existing deck; scale consistently to fit. Check 1440×810 and the actual presentation size. Report rendered size, not only unscaled CSS values.
- Body 28–36px and captions at least 20px at a 1920 stage are starting points. Inspect actual density and viewing distance; do not shrink required text to satisfy a fixed slide count.
references/motion.mdlines 3 to 11
Build still first. A thing that needs animation to look finished is not finished. Then add one authored moment, not scattered effects.

## Levels
- **Still** — hover/press/focus feedback only.
- **Subtle** — entrances once per section (heading → text → picture, 60–90ms stagger, 16–32px rise with opacity), gentle parallax, smooth wheel scroll (Lenis, not on touch).
- **Cinematic** — scenes connect: the same object carries over; scrubbed and pinned timelines; camera-like moves (dolly, rack focus) in CSS 3D.
- **Spatial** — real 3D objects (Three.js), shader backgrounds (sky, matte hex, liquid glass, light fields), depth and lighting that respond to angle.

## Connected storytelling (the signature)

references/surfaces/web.mdlines 3 to 11
## Shape
- The first screen answers the visitor's first question in large type: what it is and what it does (product), who and what they do (person). One primary action as a real button.
- Sections follow the argument (claim → proof). Vary composition when the claim or evidence changes; repeat useful comparison layouts. The idea reaches the footer; the footer is a designed close, often the name at full width.
- Navigation: wordmark, 3–5 plain links, one action. No floating blurred pill unless glass is the chosen material.
- Imagery: the project's own > generated > real pictures of the subject's world (open collections, credited; `scripts/assets.mjs photo`, `references/assets.md`) > drawn from the subject in code > type and colour alone. Never a ...

## Portfolios
- Lead with the work's outcomes, not adjectives. Each project: what it is, your role, the result, the stack, in that order.
- One signature interaction that is about the person (their tool, their craft, their medium), not a generic 3D blob.
references/layout.mdlines 3 to 11
## Composition
- The task should make sense from the layout and actual content. Before adding helper text, fix hierarchy, grouping, alignment and the placement of the next action. Keep necessary labels and accessible names.
- Clean means understandable and well composed. Preserve an expressive visual world, including chosen 3D or motion, when it supports the brief; do not make every surface visually minimal.
- Each view is a poster: one dominant thing and the content it carries. Make the big thing big before anything else exists.
- Vary neighbouring views when their information differs; repeat alignment and layout when it makes comparisons easier. Rotate between: full-bleed object, split asymmetric, statement type, dense evidence, one live thing.
- The order comes from the argument (claim → proof), not the stock sequence (hero, logos, three features, testimonial, pricing, CTA). A section with no claim is deleted.
- Carry the idea into every part, including the footer, pricing, FAQ and the last slide. The commonest failure is a distinctive opening followed by stock blocks.

## Rules

- Inline SVG with `currentColor`, so icons follow the palette and pass contrast. Icon-only controls get an accessible name.

references/surfaces/graphic.mdlines 3 to 11
## Shape
- One message, readable in under two seconds at thumbnail size. Squint test at 25% scale.
- Sizes: 1080×1350 (feed portrait), 1080×1920 (story), 1200×630 (link preview), 1920×1080 (slide), 3000×2000 (cover). Keep text in the safe area (≈ 6–8% margins; stories avoid top/bottom 14%).
- Composition: a dominant object or a dominant word, never both competing. Drenched or committed colour strategies work best here.
- Type does most of the work: one family, two sizes, tight display tracking, headline ≤ 6 words.

## Build

## Choose like a designer

1. Read the existing brand, content, explicit preferences and intended viewing conditions. Preserve pinned colours; distinguish an agent's assumption from a user choice.
2. Decide what colour must communicate: the primary action, a meaningful state, categories or the subject's material. Give each colour a role before choosing its hex value. Typography, spacing and shape must support the same di...
3. Derive a small set of neutrals and semantic accents from that context, or adapt one of the presets below. The user can choose a preset, supply exact brand colours, change the material or request a quieter direction. Offer al...
4. Check actual foreground/background pairs in controls, evidence panels, selected/disabled states and themes. The supplied presets have checked ink/ground pairs; that does not certify every component or translucent layer. Body...
5. Inspect a real-content rendering. If meaning depends on a colour, also use text, shape or position. Record the user's approved direction and meaningful token roles, not an inferred personal taste.

references/review.mdlines 3 to 11
Judge the rendered result. A source scan is evidence about source patterns, not design quality. Review-only requests produce findings without modifying the artifact; an authorized polish follows Quick.

## Order

1. Read the brief and relevant source. Run `node <skill>/scripts/slop-scan.mjs <changed-files>` when Node is available. Fix new failures only when editing is authorized; explain intentional patterns and source copy that must re...
2. Use `browser.md` to inspect the rendering. No manual browser package setup is required when host tools work. Capture the relevant sizes and states; deck advances are beats, not slide counts.
3. Apply `visual-quality.md`. Record up to three concrete defects with view/state and evidence. Load `tells.md` only when a tell needs closer diagnosis.
4. For an authorized fix, repair in one batch and recheck affected views. Broaden only for a remaining concrete defect. Do not stop while hiding a critical regression.

    How it compares.

    slopless stands on other people's work. This is what it took from each project and what it adds. It is not a benchmark: nothing was run head to head, and each project has moved since.

    With no skill

    • The model alone

      Left to its defaults it produces the average of everything it has read: centred hero, eyebrow label, three icon cards, a violet gradient. Nothing checks the result or remembers what you said last time.

    What slopless adapted

    • Impeccable

      Craft floor checks, refinement versus redesign, colour strategies, training-data font defaults, bounded verification passes, and the bolder, quieter, distill and harden modes. Apache-2.0.

    • taste-skill

      The subject inventory and idea sentence, the competitor test, one live thing, a dated tells registry, "removal is not enough" and the look-and-fix screenshot loop. MIT.

    • UI/UX Pro Max

      Priority-ordered UX rules, platform touch targets and native-app polish, a persisted design-system pattern with master and overrides, and density and motion dials. MIT.

    What slopless adds

    • Story-first interview

      Round one asks what it is, who sees it and the one sentence, before any taste question. interview.md

    • Presenter-controlled decks

      Arrow keys play the next beat and stop; the presenter owns the pace. deck.md

    • Taste memory

      Your corrections become dated lines in a file that outranks the skill's defaults on the next run. memory.md

    • Opt-in agents with model tiers

      Offered once on big jobs, one to four helpers, cheaper models for mechanical work. agents.md

    • Glass when you choose it

      Banned as decoration, allowed as a chosen material with a recipe. tells.md

    • One router, references on demand

      A short SKILL.md loads one small file per step, so a small fix never pulls in the library. SKILL.md

    • A bundled scanner

      The same file runs from the command line and in the scanner above. slop-scan.mjs

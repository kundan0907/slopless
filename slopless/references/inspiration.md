## Researching a technique
1. Open it in a real browser; note what moves, what triggers it, how long it takes, what happens on a phone and with reduced motion.
2. Inspect: CSS 3D, canvas, WebGL or video? Which library?
3. Write it down as a decision ("frosted card, 1px rim, top highlight, 18px blur, no glow") before coding.
4. Adapt it to the story and palette; say what was adopted versus built.

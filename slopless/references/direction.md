# Direction

Decide what is already true, find the idea, then commit to a world. Do not blend a direction with the layout you would have made anyway.

## 1. What is already true

- **Existing brand or design system:** inherit it. Map its colours and faces onto the roles in `palettes.md`; take only composition and motion from here.
- **Refinement:** keep identity, behaviour, copy and everything outside scope.
- **Redesign:** keep content, facts, function and constraints; treat the old look as evidence, not authority. Never split the difference by polishing the rejected look.
- **Nothing exists:** create the world with the user.

## 2. Inventory the subject

Write 8–12 things that belong to this subject and no competitor: its name and what it means, its place, its numbers, the constraint it lives with, its materials and colours, its rituals and hours, the printed or on-screen artefacts of its world (ticket, receipt, PR, dashboard, label, timetable). The idea comes from this list, not from the category.

## 3. Find the idea

> On this <surface>, **<something from the inventory>** becomes **<the device that carries the story>**.

Places to look: its output (show the real output as material), its object or shape (enlarge, crop, make it the frame), its ritual (a sequence the page or deck performs), its artefact (the ticket, the PR, the alert card), its opposite.

**Competitor test:** put the nearest competitor in the sentence. If it still works, the idea belongs to the category; find another.

**One live thing.** Give it one thing the viewer can do or watch that uses real content: drag the slider and see the real cost; watch the ticket move through the real pipeline; switch the theme and see the product re-skin. It works on a phone, has a no-script fallback and never blocks reading.

## 4. Colour strategy, then colours

Pick the strategy before the hex values:
- **Restrained** — tinted neutrals plus one accent. Default for apps, docs and dense decks.
- **Committed** — one saturated colour owns 30–60% of the surface.
- **Drenched** — the surface is the colour.
- **Full palette** — 3–4 named roles, each with a job (data, categories).

Then pick or derive a palette (`palettes.md`). Choose dark or light from where it will be seen (projector in a lit room → light or high-contrast dark; phone at night → dark), never from the category.

Calibration: generated work clusters around a few looks: cream ground + serif + terracotta; near-black + one neon + glow; hairline broadsheet + mono labels; flat saturated ink + hard offset shadows. All are fine when the brief asks. Landing in one by default means you were not deciding.

## 5. Record it

Write the direction into the brief (`brief.md`) and the durable parts (palette, type, material, motion level, signature move, what was rejected and why) into `.slopless/DESIGN.md`. Never ship this text inside the artifact.
